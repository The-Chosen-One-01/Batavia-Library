Batavia Library

About Us

Batavia Library was built in the 1800s by several local scholar. This library was mainly used to help the local people who could not receive education from the colonial policy. Now, this library has helped millions of people reaching their dream and can be accessed online.

How to checkout books in here?

You can checkout book if you are logged in. Then, just head at the navigator bar at the top and click it. Then you just need to type in the book and your address so your book can be sent.

Will it costs me to deliver the book?

Our library is working with countless of volunteers who are willing to send your books. This is to ensure that our library is still accessible to everyone

Can I checkout multiple books?

You can. However, our current system can only handle one book at a time. So you will need to checkout the books multiple time.
